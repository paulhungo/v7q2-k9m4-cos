Section 1 — The Mandate

You are the Chief of Staff — the right hand of the Executive Director, H.E. Amb. Rev. Dr. Abena Tay. She carries vision, voice and relationships; you carry the system: turning vision into schedules, documents, follow-ups, programs and measurable results.

One-line job descriptionProtect the Founder's time, run the Foundation's machinery, convert every activity into recorded, measurable progress.

You decide alone:

  • Daily scheduling & calendar management
  • Task assignment to staff/volunteers
  • Follow-ups with partners & funders
  • Filing, records, documentation systems
  • Drafting letters & responses (she approves before sending)

Needs her sign-off:

  • Any spending or financial commitment
  • Signing contracts or MOUs
  • Public statements, press interviews
  • New program launches/closures
  • Anything involving her name, image or voice

Section 5 — Founder's Office

Calendar rules

  • One calendar — nothing confirmed until it is IN the calendar and she has seen it
  • Protect her focus blocks (ministry, rest, family); decline gracefully on her behalf
  • Every Friday: present next week's movement plan for approval

Briefing card (night before — max 5 bullets)

  • WHO she meets (names, titles, what they want)
  • WHY she is there (one goal)
  • WHAT she says (key message + one story)
  • WHAT to avoid (sensitive topics)
  • WHAT happens after (photo, next step, follow-up owner)

Booking pipeline

  1. Inquiry logged → 2. Availability checked → 3. Founder confirms → 4. EPK sent → 5. Prep brief 3 days before → 6. Delivery + capture evidence → 7. Thank-you within 48h, archive recording

Protocol quick reference

  • Written: H.E. Amb. Rev. Dr. Abena Tay · Spoken: "Her Excellency, Ambassador Reverend Dr. Abena Tay"
  • Arrive 15–30 min early; you manage her entrance
  • Gifts: log in movement card · Press at events: route through you

Section 7 — Program & Event Playbook

T-minus 4 weeks — Planning

  • ONE goal + target numbers (e.g., "200 schoolgirls, 4 schools")
  • Budget drafted → Founder approval → funds confirmed
  • Date/venue locked; permissions sought (schools, prisons, assembly)
  • Invitations out; registration method open + tracker started

T-minus 2 weeks — Build

  • Materials ordered · Run sheet hour-by-hour · Roles assigned (MC, registration desk, photography, logistics, safety)
  • Media invited; press release drafted; confirmations re-sent

T-minus 3 days — Lock

  • Final headcount · Materials physically checked · Team briefed · Founder briefing card delivered · Backups (rain, power, transport, no-show speaker)

Evidence capture (non-negotiable)

  • Signed attendance sheet with counts
  • Photos (wide, action, group) WITH consent — minors need guardian/school consent
  • Video clips 30–60s · Receipts filed same week
  • Numbers recorded SAME DAY: beneficiaries reached, items distributed

T-plus 1 week — Convert

  • Post-event report within 7 days · Thank-yous · SDG translation into Impact Log · Media published · Lessons noted

Instant readiness check: Program Readiness page.

Section 13 — Difficult Times Protocol

Cash crunch

  • Triage: committed program promises → critical field needs → admin costs. Cut bottom-up, never break public promises.
  • Bridges: phased delivery; board/trusted-partner bridge; accelerate nearest stage-4 opportunity. Communicate EARLY.

Funder goes silent

  • Nudge day 7 → day 14 → monthly. Park after two nudges; keep warm (share wins); review parked quarterly.

Program fails

  • 48-hour blame-free review → lessons documented → playbook adjusted → transparent report to Founder with fix attached.

Reputation attack

  • Never respond fast/angry; gather facts (24h). ONE spokesperson: the Founder via approved statement. Correct facts, no fights. Log all.

You are overwhelmed

  • PIF filter: Programs / Income / Founder — if no, it waits. Delegate with deadline + definition of done. Tell the Founder early.

Q&A Feed

Q: What do I actually do on my very first morning?
A: Open the Today page. Do the 7-item morning routine top to bottom. Then give the Founder her 5-line brief (template in Handbook → Templates). That's it — the system carries the rest.
Q: An invitation just arrived for Madam — what do I do?
A: Run it through the Booking Pipeline (Handbook §5): log it → check calendar → hold date → ask her to confirm terms → send EPK if confirmed → prepare briefing card 3 days before → capture evidence after.
Q: A funder replied — now what?
